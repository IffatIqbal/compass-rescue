The controller reconstructs activity from eight noisy EPG rate measurements, estimates lost recurrent input, and projects compensation onto three bounded stimulation channels using a fixed nominal circuit model. It never receives true heading, turn velocity, or the intact circuit’s state.

Targets are groups of identified neurons in anatomical phase sectors. The selected three groups contain 39 neurons. Their body IDs are listed below. These are candidate interventions, not verified genetic driver lines. Feedback compensation does not establish permanent repair.

Methods, results limits & reproducibility

The anatomical source contains 106 EPG, PEG, PENa and PENb neurons, 1,932 directed neuron pairs and 46,842 synapses across EB and PB. The raw CSV, immutable repository commit, SHA-256 checksum and upstream license are included. Delta7 neurons were excluded by the source extraction.

Excitatory edge locations come from the measured graph. Their strengths are fitted with nonnegative, count-regularized least squares to idealized heading dynamics. This is task fitting, not calibration to physiological recordings. Supplemental population inhibition, preferred phases, rates, time constants and PENa velocity gating are assumptions. No cosine recurrent graph replaces the measured graph.

Three sector groups are selected from all 56 combinations at four gains using six training trials. The objective combines mean error, half its standard deviation, and stimulation cost. Eight untouched test trials vary initial heading, lesion strength/location, overall gain and time constant. The controller uses a fixed nominal damaged model and eight delayed, noisy EPG measurements. Reference heading and angular velocity do not enter its feedback law.

From 7–9 model seconds, stimulation and turns are off. Both hold error and hold drift are exported. A separate continuous-stimulation run quantifies sensitivity to removing this interruption. Twelve random target sets are evaluated; these and constant stimulation are not energy matched. Model tuning and stimulation do not prove recovery in a living animal.

No driver line, cross-specimen transfer or biological rescue has been validated. The eight reporter neurons and target body IDs are transparent experimental candidates, not a claim that the required independent optical access exists.

Run python prepare.py, then python experiment.py from the project folder. Open this file offline to replay the generated trials. The code, raw extract, fitted weights, provenance, unaggregated outputs and tests are included.
